Latest Changelog:

This is Android 16 with October 1st 2025 security patches

24.10.2025:
==============================
- Latest source sync
- Build still has some issues, regarding fingerprint verification in private space and other parts of the system. This will be fixed in the next update for all supported devices.

Notes:
==============================
- LineageOS-based source. Signed build.
- GApps build; expect poor performance on 2/32 and similar lower spec devices.
- GApps is the Pico package, thus Velvet (Google app) is not included. For proper function of the system, install the Google app from Google Play immediately after installing the ROM
- Always dirty flash at your own risk. If your device bootloops, revert to an older build that boots.
- Stock kernel
- Tested on Redmi 9A (dandelion) 2/32 

Known Bugs:
==============================
- Some devices show having fingerprint scanner, even though they don't (general blossom issue)
- Other than that, none. If you find any, tag me on Telegram
